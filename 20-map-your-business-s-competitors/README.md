# Map your business's competitors

Theoretical Recap Mapping your competitors is a foundational marketing exercise. It involves systematically identifying and analyzing all rival entities vying for your target customers' attention and budget. The goal is to understand your competitive landscape to refine your strategy and value proposition. This builds directly on your prior work identifying competitors and analyzing your market environment. A robust mental model for this is competitor categorisation. You must distinguish between direct competitors, indirect competitors, and substitute products and services. Direct competitors offer a similar product or service to the same target market. Indirect competitors solve the same core customer problem with a different type of solution. Substitutes are products or services from other industries that can replace the need for yours. A common mistake is focusing only on direct competitors. This leads to a narrow view of the market and missed opportunities. Another error is failing to update the map regularly as the competitive field evolves. A quality competitive map provides a clear, actionable visual and written summary, not just a list. Consider a small business selling premium coffee beans online. A direct competitor is another online specialty bean retailer. An indirect competitor could be a subscription service for ready-to-brew coffee capsules. A substitute is a high-end coffee machine that grinds beans automatically, changing the consumption habit.
