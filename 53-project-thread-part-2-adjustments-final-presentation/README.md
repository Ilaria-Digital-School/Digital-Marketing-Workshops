# Project thread (part 2): adjustments & final presentation

## Project Overview This mini-project is the direct continuation of 'Project thread (part 1): Full CM for 2 people'. Building on the peer-to-peer feedback session and the continuous iteration work already completed, you will now finalize, refine, and formally present your Customer Journey Map (CJM) and associated personas. This second phase focuses on three core actions: 1. **Integrating feedback**: Apply the insights gathered during the peer review and iteration sessions to improve the quality and relevance of your CJM and personas. 2. **KPI alignment**: Ensure that each stage of your CJM is equipped with clearly defined, measurable KPIs consistent with the metrics work completed in previous activities. 3. **Final presentation**: Deliver a structured, professional presentation of your complete work, as if pitching to a marketing team or a client. ## Context You have now covered the full lifecycle of audience understanding and journey mapping: from persona creation and CJM alignment, through automation scenario design, KPI definition, and iterative validation. This final project consolidates all of these competencies into one cohesive, presentation-ready deliverable. It directly validates your ability to produce and communicate a professional-grade strategic marketing asset.
