# Assess the website of the business you are following

Theoretical Recap A website must serve as the central hub for a company's online presence. It integrates all digital marketing channels, from organic search results and social media links to email campaigns and online ads. As the primary point of contact for many customers, its performance directly impacts business goals like generating leads and increasing sales. Key components of a strong website include clear navigation, a value proposition that speaks to the target audience, fast loading speeds, and a call to action. A common mental model for assessment is to evaluate how a site performs across four pillars: its purpose, content, technical performance, and design. These elements work together to create an effective user experience. A common mistake is to assess a website based purely on personal taste. Instead, you should evaluate it from a user's perspective and against the company's strategic objectives. For example, a local bakery's website that loads in 2 seconds and has a clear 'Order Online' button will likely convert better than one with slow, beautiful images and no obvious next step for the customer. Understand Your Framework Your goal is to conduct a structured review of the business website you selected earlier in the programme. This is not about redesigning it, but about analysing its current strengths and weaknesses using the core concepts of purpose, content, technical function, and design.
