# Check that your business meets customers' online expectations

Recap: Understanding the Digital Customer Journey Successful digital marketing begins by meeting core customer expectations during their online journey. Before making any purchase decision, customers systematically search for specific information. These are known as zero moment of truth touchpoints, where customers form their first impression. Key expectations include accurate product information, credible reviews, transparent pricing, and clear contact details. A common mental model is the information search matrix, where customers weigh informational depth against trust signals from a business. Businesses often make critical mistakes at this stage. They assume their website is only a brochure, failing to provide the detailed data customers seek. Another common error is hiding contact information or pricing, which creates friction and distrust immediately. For example, a customer researching a new laptop might visit five websites. The sites that directly show detailed specifications, multiple verified reviews, clear pricing with taxes, and a phone number on every page will win the initial consideration. The others will be abandoned. Introduction to Your Workshop Task This workshop asks you to put yourself in your future customer's shoes.
