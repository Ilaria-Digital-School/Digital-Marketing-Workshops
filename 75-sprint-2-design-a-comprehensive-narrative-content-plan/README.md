# Sprint 2: Design a comprehensive narrative content plan

## Project Overview In this mini-project, you will design a complete narrative content plan for a real or fictitious brand of your choice. Building directly on the skills validated in your recent activities (editorial workflow, content calendar auditing, SEO, KPIs, storytelling, and marketing automation), you will consolidate them into one coherent, operational deliverable. The goal is to move from isolated concepts to a strategic, actionable content plan that a marketing team could implement immediately. You will define audience needs, craft a storytelling angle, structure an editorial calendar, integrate SEO best practices, connect content to measurable KPIs, and design at least one automation-based nurturing scenario. ## Context A content plan is the backbone of any content marketing strategy. It aligns business objectives, audience expectations, editorial production, distribution channels, and performance measurement. This sprint requires you to demonstrate that you can articulate these elements into a single, professional-grade plan. ## Scope You will work on ONE brand or product. Choose something you understand well (a company you follow, a personal project, or a provided persona). Your plan must cover a 3-month editorial horizon. ## Skills Validated - Implement a webmarketing strategy - Organize the operational application of a webmarketing communication - Understand the needs of your targets - Set up a marketing automation strategy - Set up marketing automation scenarios adapted to strategic goals - Use tools for measuring and managing digital performance - Monitor and evaluate the results of a webmarketing communication
