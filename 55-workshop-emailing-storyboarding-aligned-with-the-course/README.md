# Workshop: emailing storyboarding aligned with the course

**Theoretical Recap** An emailing storyboard is a visual and narrative planning tool that maps out a sequence of emails sent to a specific audience segment across the customer journey. Unlike a simple editorial calendar, a storyboard aligns each email's message, tone, and call-to-action with the psychological and behavioral state of the recipient at a given stage — awareness, consideration, decision, retention, or advocacy. The key principle here is coherence: every email must feel like a natural continuation of where the persona currently stands in their journey, not a generic broadcast. A common mistake beginners make is treating all subscribers the same, sending identical messages regardless of where individuals are in the journey. Another frequent error is writing emails that focus on the brand's agenda rather than the persona's needs, pain points, and emotional expectations — elements you have already mapped in previous activities. A well-built storyboard asks: Who is this person right now? What do they feel? What do they need to hear to move forward? Mini example: For a persona named "Career-Changer Clara" at the Consideration stage, an email storyboard might plan a sequence of three emails — first, a testimonial from someone with a similar profile who succeeded; second, a comparison of program options addressing her specific hesitations; third, a limited-time offer tied to her urgency trigger.
