# Set up monitoring of your market

Theoretical Recap Market monitoring is an organised, continuous process of collecting, analysing, and interpreting information about your business environment to inform strategic decisions. It involves tracking customers, competitors, industry trends, and external forces like technological shifts or new regulations. The core mental model is a continuous feedback loop: you define your scope and key questions, you systematically gather data from selected sources, you analyse the information to identify patterns and insights, and you then adjust your strategy and refine your monitoring scope. This cycle prevents strategic surprises and enables proactive action. A common mistake is attempting to monitor everything, which leads to data overload and paralysis. Another is treating monitoring as a sporadic task rather than an embedded routine. A mini example: a local e-commerce site selling eco-friendly products could monitor its market size by estimating the number of online shoppers in its region interested in sustainability (e.g., 100,000 potential customers) and then track key trends like new competitors entering its niche (e.g., 2 new direct competitors this quarter) and changes in consumer sentiment towards plastic packaging (% of social media mentions that are negative). This workshop builds directly on your recent lessons analysing market components, estimating size, and identifying environmental changes. You will define a focused scope, select practical tools and sources, and establish a routine for turning information into strategic insights....
