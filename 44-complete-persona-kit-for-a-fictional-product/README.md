# Complete persona kit for a fictional product

## Overview This mini-project is the capstone activity for the 'Understanding and segmenting your audiences: personas and customer journey mapping' chapter. You have already explored persona creation methods, user research techniques, segmentation strategies, and documentation best practices. Now it is time to consolidate everything into a professional, ready-to-use deliverable: a **Complete Persona Kit** for a fictional product of your choice. This kit must demonstrate your ability to move from raw audience data to structured, actionable personas that could realistically guide digital marketing decisions in a professional context. --- ## What is a Complete Persona Kit? A persona kit is a structured set of documents that provides a marketing or product team with a deep, shared understanding of their target users. It goes beyond a simple profile sheet: it includes research foundations, segmentation logic, persona cards, and usage guidelines — everything a team needs to apply personas operationally. --- ## Fictional Product You will invent a fictional digital product (examples: a wellness mobile app, a B2B SaaS tool for freelancers, an e-learning platform for seniors, a sustainable fashion marketplace). The product must be realistic enough to allow serious audience segmentation and persona construction. --- ## Skills Validated - Conducting and synthesizing user research (even simulated/secondary) - Applying segmentation criteria (demographic, psychographic, behavioral, geographic) - Building structured, motivations-driven personas - Distinguishing segment logic from persona logic and combining both - Documenting and presenting personas for team use - Connecting personas to digital marketing strategy (channels, messages, automation triggers)
