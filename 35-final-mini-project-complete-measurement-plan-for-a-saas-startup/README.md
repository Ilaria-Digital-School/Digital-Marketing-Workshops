# Final mini-project: Complete measurement plan for a SaaS startup

## Project Overview This final mini-project is the capstone activity of the 'Define your SMART goals and digital KPIs' chapter. It challenges you to consolidate everything you have learned — SMART goal-setting, OKR frameworks, KPI selection, data visualization, tracking frequencies, and alert thresholds — into a single, professional-grade measurement plan for a realistic SaaS startup scenario. ## Business Scenario You are joining **CloudSync**, a B2B SaaS startup that provides project management software for remote teams. The company has just completed its seed funding round and is entering a growth phase. The CMO has tasked you with building a complete digital marketing measurement plan from scratch. CloudSync currently has: - 1,200 monthly active users (MAU) - A freemium model with a 4% free-to-paid conversion rate - A monthly churn rate of 6% - Active channels: SEO blog, LinkedIn Ads, email nurturing, and a product demo landing page - No formalized tracking or reporting system in place Your job is to design a measurement plan that gives the marketing team full visibility into performance, supports strategic decision-making, and aligns with the company's growth ambitions. ## Skills Validated This project directly validates your ability to: - Formulate SMART goals aligned with business objectives - Structure OKRs and convert them into actionable KPIs - Select and define relevant digital marketing KPIs per channel - Design a monitoring cadence (weekly vs. monthly) adapted to each metric - Set alert thresholds and define corrective action triggers - Present data-viz-ready KPI dashboards with clear reading logic -...
