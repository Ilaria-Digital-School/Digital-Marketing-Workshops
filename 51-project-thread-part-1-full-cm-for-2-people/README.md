# Project thread (part 1): Full CM for 2 people

## Project Overview This mini-project is the first part of your course thread project. It consolidates everything you have covered so far: audience segmentation, persona creation, customer journey mapping (CJM), emotions and expectations integration, KPI definition, and alignment with marketing automation logic. You will build a complete, professional-grade Customer Mapping (CM) for **two distinct personas** operating within the same brand or product ecosystem. This deliverable will serve as the strategic foundation for subsequent parts of the course thread (automation scenarios, content strategy, performance dashboards). ## Project Scenario You are a Digital Marketing Consultant hired by a company of your choice (real or fictitious). The company wants to better understand its two main customer profiles in order to optimize its digital communication and prepare a marketing automation strategy. Your mission is to deliver a full Customer Mapping document covering both personas, from discovery to post-purchase loyalty. ## Skills Validated - Constructing data-driven, realistic personas - Mapping full customer journeys across all stages (Awareness, Consideration, Decision, Retention, Advocacy) - Integrating emotions, pain points, and expectations at each touchpoint - Identifying and prioritizing optimization opportunities per stage - Assigning relevant KPIs and metrics to each stage of the journey - Aligning the CJM structure with marketing automation and content strategy logic
