# Workshop: Turning a vague goal into a SMART goal

**Theoretical Recap** Before diving into the workshop, let's consolidate what you've learned. A SMART goal is a goal that meets five criteria: Specific (clearly defined, no ambiguity), Measurable (quantified with concrete indicators), Achievable (realistic given your resources and constraints), Relevant (aligned with your broader business strategy), and Time-bound (with a clear deadline). In digital marketing, vague goals like 'increase our online visibility' or 'get more traffic' are extremely common — and almost useless. They give no direction, no metric for success, and no accountability. The most frequent mistake beginners make is confusing an intention with a goal. 'We want more social media engagement' is an intention. 'Increase our Instagram engagement rate from 1.2% to 3.5% within 90 days by publishing 5 posts per week with video content' is a SMART goal. Notice how the second version tells you exactly what to do, how to measure it, and when to achieve it. Another common error is setting goals that sound SMART on the surface but are not actually achievable or relevant — for example, aiming for a 500% traffic increase in two weeks with no budget. Always sanity-check each criterion.
