# Workshop: Choosing the right visual for each KPI

**Theoretical Recap** A dashboard is only as good as the visuals it uses to represent data. Choosing the wrong chart type for a KPI is one of the most common mistakes in digital marketing reporting — it creates confusion, slows decision-making, and undermines the credibility of your analysis. The core principle is simple: every visual must serve the data, not decorate it. Here are the key chart types and their primary use cases. A **line chart** is ideal for tracking trends over time (e.g., weekly sessions, monthly revenue). A **bar chart** (vertical or horizontal) is best for comparing discrete categories (e.g., traffic by channel, conversion rate by campaign). A **pie or donut chart** is suited only when you need to show proportions of a whole — and only when you have fewer than 5–6 segments. A **gauge or KPI card** works well for a single metric against a target (e.g., current NPS score vs. goal). A **funnel chart** is specifically designed for sequential conversion steps. A **scatter plot** reveals correlations between two variables (e.g., ad spend vs. revenue). Common mistakes include using pie charts for time-series data, stacking too many lines on one chart, or using 3D visuals that distort perception. A golden rule: if your audience needs more than 5 seconds to understand a visual, it is wrong.
