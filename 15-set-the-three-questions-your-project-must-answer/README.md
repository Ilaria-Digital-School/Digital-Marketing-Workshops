# Set the three questions your project must answer

Theory Recap: The Core Questions for Strategic Direction Every digital marketing project needs a clear strategic direction from the very beginning. Steering a project without defining its core questions is like navigating without a map. The fundamental three questions you must set serve as your strategic compass, ensuring all subsequent actions align with a clear purpose. These questions are distinct from tactical goals like increasing followers. They are the high-level inquiries that your entire project exists to resolve. A common mental model is to view them as the pillars supporting your marketing strategy, bridging your initial business context analysis with your specific, measurable objectives. Key definitions are crucial. Strategic questions are broad, foundational, and focused on understanding or achieving a fundamental business shift. In contrast, Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are the measurable metrics used to answer whether you are succeeding. Common mistakes include asking purely tactical questions or creating questions that are too vague to guide meaningful analysis. Consider a small numeric example. A boutique selling handmade ceramics might ask: 'What is the potential revenue from online sales in the UK market?' This leads to analyzing market size, average order values, and online shopper percentages, moving you from a general idea to a quantifiable target. Setting the wrong question, like 'How do I get more Instagram likes?' misdirects resources from the core business goal.
