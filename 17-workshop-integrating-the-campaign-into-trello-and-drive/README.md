# Workshop: integrating the campaign into Trello and Drive

**Theoretical Recap** A digital marketing campaign does not exist in a vacuum. It is a structured sequence of tasks, assets, deadlines, and stakeholders that must be coordinated to deliver a coherent message to the right audience at the right time. Two tools make this coordination manageable: Trello and Google Drive. Trello is a visual project management tool based on the Kanban method — you organize work into boards, lists, and cards, each representing a stage or a task. Google Drive is a cloud-based document storage and collaboration platform where you centralize all campaign assets (briefs, visuals, copy, reports). Together, they form a simple but powerful operational backbone. A common mistake beginners make is treating these tools as separate silos: they plan in Trello but store files chaotically in Drive, or vice versa. The mental model to adopt is this: Trello is your campaign's nervous system (it tracks what needs to happen, by whom, and when), while Drive is its memory (it stores everything that is produced). Every Trello card should, where relevant, link directly to a Drive document. This creates a single source of truth. Mini example: A card titled 'Write welcome email copy' in Trello should contain a direct link to the Google Doc where the copy is being written, a due date, a checklist of sub-tasks (draft, review, validate), and the name of the person responsible.
