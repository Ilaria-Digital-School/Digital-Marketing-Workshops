# Mini-project: designing a simple email campaign plan

## Overview This mini-project is the capstone activity of the introductory chapter 'First steps in digital marketing and setting up the work environment.' You have already explored Google Analytics, KPIs, audience personas, value propositions, monitoring tools (Google Alerts, Feedly), and personal organization methods (Pomodoro, Trello). Now you will bring all of these elements together by designing a complete, actionable email campaign plan for a product or service of your choice (real or fictional). This is not a theoretical exercise. You are expected to produce a concrete, professional-grade document that demonstrates your ability to think strategically, understand a target audience, define measurable goals, and organize the operational execution of a digital marketing campaign. --- ## Context and Rationale Email marketing remains one of the highest-ROI channels in digital marketing. Knowing how to plan an email campaign — from audience definition to performance measurement — is a foundational skill for any digital marketing professional. This mini-project simulates a real workplace task: a junior digital marketer being asked to draft a campaign plan to present to their team or client. By completing this project, you will consolidate the skills developed throughout this introductory chapter and demonstrate readiness to move into more advanced digital marketing topics.
