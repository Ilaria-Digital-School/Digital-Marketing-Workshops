# Your project's starting file: business, online presence and organisation

Recap the foundations of your starting point A starting file is the photograph of your business on day one, before you change anything. It has three layers: the business itself (what it sells, to whom, how it earns money), its online presence (every place where the public can find it on the internet) and your organisation (the time, money and tools you can put into the project). Without this photograph, you will never be able to prove that an action worked, because you will have nothing to compare it with. The mental model is simple: test, measure, adjust. Every later decision in this programme starts from a baseline figure, a date and a source. A figure without a date or a source cannot be compared over time, so it is almost useless. Three mistakes come up often. You list channels without checking whether they are active. You write rounded figures from memory instead of reading them in the tool. You describe what you would like to do instead of what exists today. Mini example: a fictional bakery records, on 3 March, 1,200 monthly website visits read in its analytics tool, 850 Instagram followers, an average rating of 4.3 out of 5 on 62 reviews, 3 hours a week spent on digital and 40 euros a month for its website hosting.
