# Sprint 1: define goals and personas for a personal project

## Overview This mini-project is the first concrete sprint of your content marketing journey. Building on everything you have practiced so far — persona creation, user journey mapping, content-stage alignment, and strategy diagnosis — you will now apply these skills to a real personal project of your choosing. You will define the strategic foundations of a content marketing strategy: clear, measurable goals and one or more well-constructed personas. This sprint simulates the actual first phase of any professional content strategy and is designed to validate your ability to move from theory to action. ## Project Description Choose a personal project for which you want to develop a content marketing strategy. This can be: - A freelance service you want to offer (e.g., graphic design, copywriting, coaching) - A side business or e-commerce project - A personal brand or professional repositioning project - A blog, newsletter, podcast, or YouTube channel - A non-profit initiative or community project Your project must be real or realistically plausible. Avoid purely fictional scenarios. The more genuine your project, the more useful this sprint will be to your career transition.
