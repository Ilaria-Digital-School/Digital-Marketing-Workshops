# Add your competitors and their platforms to your monitoring

Theoretical Recap Effective market monitoring hinges on competitor analysis and technological surveillance. This involves systematically tracking competitive moves and the platforms they use to reach customers. A clear mental model is the Competitive Landscape Map, a structured visual representation of your rivals and their market positions across key digital touchpoints. Common mistakes include focusing only on direct competitors, ignoring emerging channels, and collecting data without analysis. Many marketers also overload their monitoring with irrelevant metrics, leading to alert fatigue. The goal is actionable intelligence. Consider a mini example: An eco-friendly sneaker brand identifies five competitors. They create a simple table tracking each competitor's social platforms (X, Instagram, TikTok), content themes, and engagement rates. This reveals that Competitor A performs best on TikTok with user-generated content campaigns, prompting the brand to reconsider its channel mix. Workshop Brief This workshop requires you to update your competitive monitoring system. Based on your previous analyses of two competitors, you will now operationalize this knowledge into active surveillance. You will define a practical framework for tracking their digital activities. The objective is to move from static analysis to dynamic monitoring. Your system should allow you to detect strategic shifts, marketing campaign launches, and platform performance changes. You will integrate this step into your existing market monitoring routine established in previous activities. Your deliverable will be a structured, actionable monitoring plan.
