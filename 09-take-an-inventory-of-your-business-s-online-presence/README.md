# Take an inventory of your business's online presence

Theoretical recap Your online presence is every place on the internet where your business appears, whether you control it or not. A useful mental model sorts each touchpoint into three families. Owned media are the spaces you control, such as your website, social accounts and newsletter. Earned media are what others publish about you, such as reviews, press articles, forum mentions and directory listings. Paid media are the spaces you pay for, such as ads and sponsored listings. Each touchpoint also serves a stage of the customer journey: attract, convert or retain. You saw in previous lessons that customers look online for four things: information, reviews, prices and a way to make contact. An inventory checks whether your touchpoints actually provide these, and it measures them with numbers rather than impressions. The most common mistakes are easy to avoid once you know them. Many people list only the accounts they created and forget what others publish. Others count accounts without checking whether they are active and accurate. Some ignore inconsistent details, such as two different phone numbers. Finally, many judge quality by feeling instead of recording figures. Here is a mini example. A florist believes her presence is fine because she has a website and an Instagram account. Her inventory reveals three problems: an old directory listing shows her previous address, her Google profile has 12 unanswered reviews, and her website shows no prices.
