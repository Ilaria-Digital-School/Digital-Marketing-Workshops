# Capstone: Presentation and arguments of its content marketing strategy

This capstone project is the culmination of the 'Fundamentals of content marketing and storytelling' chapter. You will consolidate everything you have learned — from defining objectives, building a narrative content plan, associating KPIs with goals, and designing automation scenarios — into a single, coherent content marketing strategy that you must present and defend. The goal is simple and concrete: you will play the role of a content marketing consultant pitching a complete strategy to a client (real or fictional brand of your choice). You must not only produce the strategy document, but also present it and argue your choices convincingly, as you would in a real professional context. Section 1 — Context: Choose a brand (existing, fictional, or your own project). Define its industry, its target audience, and its business context. This becomes the backbone of your strategy. Section 2 — Strategy: Build a content marketing strategy that includes clear objectives, an understanding of the target audience, a narrative/storytelling angle, a content plan (formats and editorial calendar), the KPIs used to measure performance, and at least one marketing automation / nurturing scenario. Section 3 — Presentation & argumentation: Prepare a pitch (slides + oral delivery or recorded video) where you present your strategy and justify each decision. This is where you demonstrate that you can not only design a strategy but also defend it with solid arguments tied to objectives and expected results.
