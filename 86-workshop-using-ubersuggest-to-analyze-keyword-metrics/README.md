# Workshop: using Ubersuggest to analyze keyword metrics

THEORETICAL RECAP Keyword research is the foundation of any SEO strategy. In the previous lessons, you learned to brainstorm keywords with Google Suggest and to distinguish search intents (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial). Now you must learn to quantify those keywords using concrete metrics. Ubersuggest is a freemium tool that exposes four essential metrics. Search Volume estimates how many monthly searches a keyword receives. SEO Difficulty (SD) measures how hard it is to rank in organic results on a 0-100 scale. Paid Difficulty (PD) reflects competition in paid ads. CPC (Cost Per Click) indicates the commercial value advertisers assign to the term. The correct mental model: a good target keyword balances decent volume, achievable difficulty, and clear intent alignment with your content. Do not chase the highest-volume keyword available. A term with 90,000 searches and an SD of 78 is unreachable for a young site, while a 'long-tail' keyword with 500 searches and an SD of 12 can drive qualified traffic quickly. Common mistakes: obsessing over volume alone, ignoring intent, and confusing high CPC (commercial value) with ease of ranking. Another frequent error is treating estimated metrics as absolute truth; they are directional indicators, not certainties. Mini example: for a blog on home fitness, 'workout' (huge volume, very high SD) is a trap, but '15 minute home workout for beginners' (moderate volume, low SD, clear informational intent) is a realistic, high-value target.
