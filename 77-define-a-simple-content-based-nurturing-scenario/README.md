# Define a simple content-based nurturing scenario

THEORETICAL RECAP Lead nurturing is the process of guiding a prospect through their buying journey by delivering the right content at the right time, until they are ready to convert. A nurturing scenario is an automated sequence of content (usually emails) triggered by a specific action, such as downloading a resource or subscribing to a newsletter. The mental model to keep in mind is a funnel divided into three stages: awareness (the prospect discovers a problem), consideration (they compare solutions), and decision (they choose a provider). Each piece of content must match the stage the prospect is in. A simple scenario connects a trigger, a series of messages, waiting delays between them, and a goal. For example: a visitor downloads an ebook on 'social media basics' (trigger). On day 0 they receive a welcome email with the ebook; on day 3, an article on a related challenge; on day 6, a case study; on day 9, an invitation to book a demo (goal). Common mistakes include sending content that is too promotional too early, ignoring the funnel stage, spacing emails poorly (too fast feels aggressive, too slow loses interest), and having no measurable goal. A good nurturing scenario is relevant, progressive, and tied to a clear KPI such as the conversion rate to the next stage.
