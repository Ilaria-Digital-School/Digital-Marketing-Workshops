# Identify the digital marketing actions of a brand you know

Recap: From Concepts to Concrete Actions Digital marketing uses digital channels to promote products and services. It differs from traditional marketing through its capacity for precise targeting, real-time measurement, and direct interaction. The core objective remains the same as all marketing: to generate value for a brand and its customers. Your previous lessons covered the distinction between marketing, communication, and sales. Marketing defines the value proposition and strategic direction. Communication creates and broadcasts messages to audiences. Sales close the transaction. Digital marketing blends these functions on new platforms. A foundational mental model is the marketing funnel: Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action (AIDA). In a perfect digital strategy, your actions guide users from discovering your brand to making a purchase. A common mistake is to focus all efforts on the bottom of the funnel, like promotion codes, without building the top-of-funnel awareness necessary to attract new customers. Another is neglecting consistent measurement of key performance indicators (KPIs). Imagine a small online bakery. Its digital marketing actions could include: using SEO so its site appears when people search for "best birthday cake delivery" (Awareness), posting photos of its creations on Instagram (Interest), sending an email newsletter with a special offer for subscribers (Desire), and a Google Ads campaign targeting the keyword "order cake online" for immediate clicks (Action).
