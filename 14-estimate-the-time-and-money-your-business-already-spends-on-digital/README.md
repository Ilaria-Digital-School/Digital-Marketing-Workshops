# Estimate the time and money your business already spends on digital

Theoretical Recap: Understanding Your Digital Investment Before you start calculating, you must understand what constitutes digital activity. Digital marketing is any marketing effort that uses an electronic device or the internet. This includes owned, earned, and paid media on all digital channels. A common mistake is to only count paid advertising spend, which leads to a significant underestimation. Many businesses invest substantial time and internal resources without tracking it. Another error is forgetting one-off or irregular activities, like annual website updates or seasonal campaigns. To build an accurate picture, you need a clear mental model: your digital investment is the sum of Money (direct costs, subscriptions, ad spend) and Time (hours valued at an internal labor cost). This is your Total Digital Cost. For a small café, this might include: $50/month on social media ads (Money), 5 hours/week by the owner managing Instagram valued at $25/hour (Time = $125/week). This mental model will be the foundation of your workshop analysis. Workshop Brief: Audit Your Current Digital Footprint Your goal in this workshop is to conduct a rigorous self-audit of your own business's current digital activities. You will move from awareness to quantification. This is the essential first step in designing a future strategy, as you cannot improve what you do not measure. You will identify every digital channel and tool your business uses, then assign realistic cost and time values to each.
