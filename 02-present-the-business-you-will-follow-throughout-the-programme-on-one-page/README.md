# Present the business you will follow throughout the programme on one page

Theoretical Recap: The Business as Your Central Case Study To design an effective digital marketing strategy, you must first deeply understand the business you are acting for. This foundational document condenses that understanding. The business's core identity—its mission, market, and competitive position—serves as the bedrock for all future strategic decisions. Key concepts include the value proposition, which is the primary reason a customer chooses you over a competitor. The target audience is the specific segment you aim to serve. Positioning is the space your brand occupies in the customer's mind relative to competitors. A common mistake is being too generic or overly comprehensive, trying to appeal to everyone, which dilutes your message and strategy. A helpful mental model is the "Onion Model" of information: start with core, foundational facts (mission, industry) in the center, then layer on operational details (products, services), and finish with the external market context (competitors, customers). For a mini example, a local bakery's value proposition might be "artisan sourdough bread baked daily." Its target audience could be health-conscious urbanites aged 25-45. Its key performance indicators might be weekly online order volume and customer satisfaction score. You will work on this single entity for all subsequent workshops to ensure continuity and practical learning.
