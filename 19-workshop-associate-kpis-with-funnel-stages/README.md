# Workshop: associate KPIs with funnel stages

THEORETICAL RECAP Before diving into the practical exercise, let's consolidate the essential concepts you need to carry out this workshop confidently. The marketing funnel is a model that represents the customer journey from first contact with a brand all the way to conversion and beyond. It is traditionally divided into three main stages: Top of Funnel (TOFU), which is about awareness and attracting a broad audience; Middle of Funnel (MOFU), which focuses on engagement and nurturing interest; and Bottom of Funnel (BOFU), which is where decisions are made and conversions happen. Some models add a post-conversion stage covering loyalty and retention. A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is a measurable value that tells you whether a specific objective is being met. Not all KPIs are relevant at every stage of the funnel. This is a common and costly mistake: tracking conversion rate at the TOFU stage, for instance, is misleading because visitors at that stage are not yet ready to buy. Conversely, obsessing over traffic volume at the BOFU stage distracts from what really matters — whether leads are actually converting. Mini example: if you run a blog article to attract new visitors (TOFU), the relevant KPIs are organic traffic, impressions, and click-through rate (CTR). If you send a retargeting email to warm leads (MOFU), you track open rate, click rate, and time on page.
