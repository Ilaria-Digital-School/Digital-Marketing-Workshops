# List your business's accounts and secure access to them

Theoretical recap This practical workshop builds directly on your previous work where you identified your company's online presence. Now, you will create a master inventory of all the digital accounts associated with your business and develop a plan to secure them. Let's establish a clear mental model called an Account Footprint. This is a single source of truth listing every digital platform where your business has a registered presence or holds data. A secured footprint prevents fraud, data loss, and operational disruption. It is a cornerstone of professional digital strategy. Common mistakes include creating only a partial list, failing to include all team members with access, and securing accounts with weak, reused passwords. Many businesses operate without a clear recovery plan for lost passwords. Another frequent error is not separating personal accounts from business accounts. For example, consider a small consultancy with five core accounts: its website CMS, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, Facebook business account, and email hosting portal. Each account has one primary administrator and one backup. Without a proper inventory, if the administrator leaves, access to critical marketing and communication tools could be permanently lost. Workshop brief and objective Your task is to conduct a complete audit and produce a secure, actionable record of your business's digital identity. This workshop is the foundational step for all future digital marketing activities, ensuring you maintain control over your online channels and data.
