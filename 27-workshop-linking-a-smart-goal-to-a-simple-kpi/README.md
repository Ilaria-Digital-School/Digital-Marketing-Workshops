# Workshop: Linking a SMART goal to a simple KPI

**Theoretical Recap** A SMART goal defines where you want to go: it is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, and Time-Based. A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is the instrument you use to know whether you are actually getting there. These two tools are complementary but distinct. The goal sets the destination; the KPI measures the journey. A common mistake is to confuse them: writing 'increase our conversion rate by 10%' is already a SMART goal with a built-in metric, but selecting 'number of website visitors' as its KPI would be misaligned — visitors do not directly measure conversions. The right KPI must be logically and directly connected to what the goal is trying to achieve. Another frequent error is choosing a KPI that is too broad or too difficult to collect, making tracking impractical. A simple mental model: ask yourself 'If I track this number regularly, will it tell me unambiguously whether my goal is on track?' If yes, it is a good KPI. Mini example: SMART goal — 'Generate 200 newsletter sign-ups via our landing page within 30 days.' Aligned KPI — 'Number of new newsletter subscribers per day via landing page.' This KPI is direct, easy to read in any email marketing tool, and tells you immediately whether you are progressing toward the target. **Workshop Brief** Now that you have a solid understanding of SMART goals and what KPIs are and why they matter, it is time to build the connection between the two in a concrete, applied exercise.
