# Workshop: Rewriting an action plan with SMART goals and KPIs

**Theoretical Recap** A SMART goal is a goal that is Specific (clearly defined, focused on one precise outcome), Measurable (quantified with a concrete figure), Achievable (realistic given your resources), Relevant (aligned with a broader strategic priority), and Time-bound (attached to a clear deadline). A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is not just any metric — it is the specific data point you track to know whether your goal has been reached. KPIs belong to three main families: acquisition (measuring how you attract new visitors or leads), engagement (measuring how users interact with your content or brand), and conversion (measuring how many users take a desired action such as purchasing, signing up, or downloading). A common mistake is confusing a vague intention with a real goal. For example, 'increase our social media presence' is not a SMART goal. Rewritten as a SMART goal, it becomes: 'Increase our Instagram follower count from 2,000 to 3,500 within 3 months by publishing 5 posts per week, in order to support the launch of our new product line.' The associated KPI would be the follower growth rate (acquisition family), tracked weekly. Another frequent mistake is selecting a KPI that does not directly measure whether the goal was achieved — for instance, tracking page views when the goal is about newsletter sign-ups.
