# Mini-project: write the complete keyword brief for a fictional site

## Project Overview In this mini-project, you will produce a complete, professional-grade keyword brief for a fictional website. A keyword brief is the strategic document that guides all subsequent SEO content and structure decisions. It bridges keyword research, search intent analysis, and prioritization into a single actionable deliverable that a content team or client could execute immediately. You will apply everything covered in this chapter: brainstorming keywords with Google Suggest, understanding and classifying search intent, analyzing keyword metrics (volume, difficulty, CPC), grouping keywords by intent, and prioritizing them to identify quick wins. ## Context Choose ONE fictional site from the following scenarios (or invent your own with equivalent scope): 1. **'GreenSprout'** — an e-commerce store selling indoor plants and gardening accessories. 2. **'FitCoach Pro'** — a service site offering online personal training subscriptions. 3. **'LegalFast'** — a B2B SaaS providing automated contract generation for freelancers. 4. **'TravelNest'** — a blog and booking platform for eco-friendly travel. You will treat this site as a real client. Your keyword brief must be detailed enough that a copywriter or SEO specialist could start producing optimized content without asking you any questions. ## What is expected You will deliver a structured keyword brief document containing seeded and expanded keywords, their metrics, their classification by search intent, thematic groupings (topic clusters), a prioritization matrix, and a set of concrete content recommendations. The brief must reflect real strategic reasoning, not just a list of terms.
