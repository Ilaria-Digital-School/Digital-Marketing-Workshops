# Summary mini-project: building the complete SEO plan for a site

## Project overview This mini-project brings together everything you have covered in the SEO chapter: technical crawlability, on-page optimization, off-page strategy (netlinking and backlinks), and performance monitoring with KPIs and Google Search Console. Your mission is to build a **complete, actionable SEO plan** for a real or fictitious website of your choice (a local business, an e-commerce store, a blog, a SaaS landing page, etc.). You will act as an SEO consultant hired to audit the site, define a strategy, and produce a roadmap that a client or a team could execute. The project is designed to validate your ability to move from diagnosis to a structured, measurable action plan. ## Skills validated - Implement a webmarketing strategy through a coherent SEO plan. - Organize the operational application of an SEO communication (roadmap, priorities, timeline). - Monitor and evaluate results using measurement tools (Google Search Console, KPIs). - Understand the needs of targets through keyword and search-intent analysis. - Know and use webmarketing tools for audit, keyword research, and performance tracking. - Analyze and optimize the user journey on digital channels via on-page and technical recommendations. ## Scope of the site chosen Choose a site with at least 5 to 10 indexable pages so that your analysis has enough material. If you do not manage a real site, pick a public site you can crawl and audit externally, or build a fictitious but realistic scenario with sample pages.
