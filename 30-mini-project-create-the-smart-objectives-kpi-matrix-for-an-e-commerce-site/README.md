# Mini-project: Create the SMART objectives/KPI matrix for an e-commerce site

## Overview This mini-project is the capstone activity for the 'Define your SMART goals and digital KPIs' chapter. Having worked through AARRR funnel mapping, baseline/target calculation, and leading vs. lagging KPI identification, you are now ready to consolidate everything into a single, professional deliverable: a complete SMART objectives/KPI matrix for a fictional e-commerce site. You will take on the role of a Digital Marketing Consultant onboarded by 'ShopNova', a mid-sized e-commerce retailer selling home décor products online. The business has just relaunched its website and needs a clear performance framework before launching its first digital marketing campaigns. ## Business Context ShopNova's management has shared the following priorities for the next 6 months: - Grow website traffic from organic and paid sources - Improve the conversion rate of the online store - Increase customer retention and repeat purchases - Build brand awareness on social media Current known data (baseline): - Monthly unique visitors: 8,000 - Conversion rate: 1.2% - Average order value: €55 - Monthly revenue: €5,280 - Email open rate: 18% - Social media followers (Instagram): 3,200 - Customer retention rate (90-day): 14% ## What You Will Build You will produce a structured SMART objectives/KPI matrix that covers all relevant stages of the AARRR funnel (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue), integrating both leading and lagging indicators, with defined baselines and realistic targets.
