# Match each channel of your business to a stage of the journey

Theoretical Recap Your previous lessons covered digital marketing channels and the customer journey. It's time to connect these concepts into a strategic model. A marketing channel is any digital platform, tool, or method you use to communicate with your audience. The customer journey maps the stages a person goes through, from initial awareness of a need to becoming a loyal advocate. A clear mental model is: Channels are the *touchpoints* where you interact with prospects and customers. Each stage of the journey—Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Loyalty, Advocacy—requires different types of communication. The core of modern marketing strategy is aligning these deliberately. A common mistake is using every available channel indiscriminately, which spreads resources thin and confuses your message. Another error is using the same channel for all journey stages, like focusing solely on social media ads to build loyalty. Success depends on choosing channels where your audience naturally congregates at each stage. Consider this mini-example: A small B2B software company might use LinkedIn and industry blogs during the Awareness stage to educate. For Consideration, they deploy targeted Google Search ads and gated webinars. The Conversion stage happens on their secure website checkout. Email marketing nurtures Loyalty post-purchase.
